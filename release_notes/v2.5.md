# v2.5: Driving the M1 (stage 5)

**Download:** grab **JohnsBeerRun-v2.5.zip** below, unzip it, and import `project.godot` in Godot 4.3+.

### New
- **The red BMW M1 is drivable.** Walk up to it in the cul-de-sac and press **E** to get in. Press **E** again to get out; you have to stop first.
- **Controls:**
  - **W** is gas.
  - **S** brakes, then reverses.
  - **A/D** steer.
  - **Space** is the handbrake, for slides.
  - **H** is the horn.
- **Handling is ported from the web game.**
  - Steering tightens up at speed.
  - Being wired raises the top speed.
  - Driving drunk makes the steering wander and lag.
- **Crashes** thump, shake the camera and bounce the car back off walls, trees, houses and parked cars.
- **The car comes alive when you get in.**
  - The headlights come on and light up the road.
  - The brake lights glow when you brake.
  - The engine note revs through 4 gears.
  - The wheels spin and steer.
- **Chase camera:** it pulls back and widens with speed, and a speedometer shows on the HUD.
- **The visitor** is solid to the car, so you can't drive through him, and he still can't be hurt.
