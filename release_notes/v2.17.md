# v2.17: Ben's doorway, and Ben can be shot

**Download:** grab **JohnsBeerRun-Setup-v2.17.exe** (installer) or **JohnsBeerRun-Windows-v2.17.zip** below.

### Ben's front door opens onto his hallway
- When Ben opened his door, you used to see the house's siding behind it. Now the doorway is cut through the wall, and you see his lit hallway with the door swung inside.
- John still can't walk into Ben's house.

### Ben is no longer invincible
- John can shoot Ben, the same as the clerk and the police. Shots bleed and leave blood spatter, and shooting him brings the police.
- **Wounded:** Ben yells at John, goes back inside and shuts the door. His job is off: he won't answer the door again, and he tells John to get away from his house.
- **Killed** (a head shot, or three body shots): Ben collapses on his doorstep as a ragdoll, and the job ends.

Built and tested on Godot 4.7.2.
