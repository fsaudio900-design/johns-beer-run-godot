# v2.7 hotfix 1: car drives again, porch steps fixed

Same version (v2.7). Bug fixes only.

**Download:** grab **JohnsBeerRun-v2.7-hotfix1.zip** below, unzip it, and import `project.godot` in Godot 4.3+.

### Fixes
- **The M1 wouldn't move.** The spare beer case that rides in the trunk still had its collision box. The car kept hitting its own trunk and stayed stuck. The trunk case no longer has collision, and the car drives again.
- **You couldn't walk back up the porch steps.** John now steps up onto anything up to 35 cm high: the porch steps, curbs and the Fuel Stop's floor lip.
