# v2.11 hotfix 2: officers keep their pistols up

Same version (v2.11). Bug fix only.

**Download:** grab **JohnsBeerRun-Setup-v2.11-hotfix2.exe** (installer) or **JohnsBeerRun-Windows-v2.11-hotfix2.zip** below.

### Fix
- **In a shootout, officers held their arms half-raised against their chest and the pistol disappeared.** An officer only raised his gun while he could see John, so whenever a lamp post, pump or pillar flickered across his view he kept starting to lower and raise it. That left his arms stuck halfway with the gun hidden. Officers now draw at the start of a shootout and keep the pistol up in both hands until it's over, even while walking or when John is out of sight. They just don't fire until they can see him.
- The officers' arm joints are now reset every frame before the aim is applied, so the stance can't slowly drift out of shape during a long stand-off.
- Arms are a little straighter in the aiming stance.
