# v2.13: a bar on Main Street

**Download:** grab **JohnsBeerRun-Setup-v2.13.exe** (installer) or **JohnsBeerRun-Windows-v2.13.zip** below.

### New building: the bar
- A grimy brick bar now stands on the north side of Main Street, between the police station and the Fuel Stop.
- **The front** is the "dirty street" facade you supplied, exactly as made: the graffiti shutter, side door with its awning, pilasters and drainpipes, the neon BAR sign, the dumpster, crates and trash bags.

### The rest of the building, built to match
- **Side and back walls:** the same brick, as a seamless tile cut from the facade's own texture and colour-matched to it. The facade's metal ledge band and coping carry on round the building.
- **Back entrance:** a copy of the facade's door, awning, wall lamp and electrical box, with the dumpster wheeled round, crates, trash bags and drainpipes. The facade's white "TRASH lovers" tag appears on the side walls and the back.
- **Flat tar roof** behind a short parapet, with an AC unit, an exhaust fan, a roof hatch and vent pipes.
- **At night:** the neon sign casts a red glow on the street, and lamps light the side door and the back door.

### In the game
- John, the cars and the police can't pass through it, and police route round it.
- The facade's own sidewalk lines up with Main Street's.
- The bar can't be entered yet.

Built and tested on Godot 4.7.2.
