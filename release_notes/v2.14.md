# v2.14: the bar opens

**Download:** grab **JohnsBeerRun-Setup-v2.14.exe** (installer) or **JohnsBeerRun-Windows-v2.14.zip** below.

### Walk into the bar
- The bar's front door (the one under the awning on Main Street) now opens. Walk up and press **E** to swing it open, walk in, and press **E** again to shut it behind you, from either side.
- The door swings into the room on its hinges and shuts solid. It won't close on John while he's standing in the doorway.
- Inside, the camera stays in the room.

### A proper dive bar inside
Modelled in Blender to fit the building exactly:
- **The bar:** a long wood counter with a padded arm rest and a brass foot rail, seven red vinyl stools, and a four-handle tap tower. There are pints and empties on the bar.
- **Behind the bar:** a back-bar with coolers, a mirror and three shelves of bottles from eight made-up brands: Pine Hollow Rye, Black Bear Whiskey, Gold Rush Tequila, Capt. Barnacle Rum, Polar Vodka, Gator Gin, Moonshine XXX and Lumberjack Lager. Also a cash register and a "CASH ONLY – ATM is broken" sign.
- **Pool table:** green felt, pockets, a racked set of balls, a cue on the table, a cue rack on the wall and a long billiard lamp overhead.
- **Booths:** three red vinyl booths along the back wall, each with a pendant lamp.
- **Darts:** a dartboard in its cabinet, a taped throw line on the floor, and a chalkboard of specials.
- **Jukebox:** glowing, with a song list.
- **TV:** high in the corner, showing the ballgame.
- **Neon signs:** "Lumberjack Lager on tap" over the bottles, "COLD BEER", "Cocktails" and "OPEN".
- **Posters and signs:** a live band, pool league night, "No minors", "Staff only" and an out-of-order restroom.
- **The room itself:** worn plank floor, wood wainscoting with dark brick above, a pressed-tin ceiling with beams and an exposed duct.
- There are doors to the storeroom (behind the shutter), the restroom and the back alley exit.

### Lighting
- The room is lit warm and dim: pendant lamps over the bar and booths, the pool table lamp, and the glow of the neon, jukebox and TV.
- The lamps by the walls and the bar's outside lamps now cast shadows, so light no longer shows through the brick in either direction.

Built and tested on Godot 4.7.2.
