# v2.15: no more late-night visitor

**Download:** grab **JohnsBeerRun-Setup-v2.15.exe** (installer) or **JohnsBeerRun-Windows-v2.15.zip** below.

### Removed: the visitor
- The man in the hat and suit no longer comes to John's front door. Nobody knocks, and nobody asks for number 14.
- His knocking, his conversation with John, the "Answer the door" prompt and John's lines about him are gone.
- His model and walking routes have been taken out of the game.

### Unchanged
- The front door peephole still works: press it at the front door to look out at the cul-de-sac, then press E to open the door from there.
- The Fuel Stop clerk and the police are unaffected.

Built and tested on Godot 4.7.2.
