# v2.16: Ben across the street

**Download:** grab **JohnsBeerRun-Setup-v2.16.exe** (installer) or **JohnsBeerRun-Windows-v2.16.zip** below.

### New neighbour: Ben
- Ben lives in the house directly across the cul-de-sac from John.
- **Knock on his door:** walk up to his front door and press **E**. His door swings open and he steps out onto the step.
- **Talk to him:** press **E** again to start a conversation. Press E during the conversation to skip to the next line.
- Ben is fully animated: he idles, looks around, talks with his hands, nods, laughs and shrugs. He turns to face John while they talk, and goes back inside if John wanders off.

### Ben's job
- Ben asks John to rob the Fuel Stop on Main Street and bring him the money.
- The hint at the bottom of the screen tracks the job: "rob the Fuel Stop", then "bring the money to Ben".
- Rob the register, the same as before, then knock on Ben's door and choose **Give Ben the money**. Ben takes the till's takings and hands John a 25% cut.
- If John already robbed the store before talking to Ben, Ben just wants it handed over.
- If the police are after John, Ben won't open the door.

Built and tested on Godot 4.7.2.
