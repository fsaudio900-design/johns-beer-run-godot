# v2.10: new cashier and police officers

**Download:** grab **JohnsBeerRun-Setup-v2.10.exe** (installer) or **JohnsBeerRun-Windows-v2.10.zip** below.

### New characters
- **The Fuel Stop cashier** is now the realistic "Cashier Lady" model (lilac top, dark trousers, hair in a bun). She greets you, waves, puts her hands up during a robbery, spins and drops to a knee when hit, and collapses as a full-body ragdoll, same as before.
- **Police officers** are now the realistic officer in uniform and sunglasses. They walk, chase and arrest you with the same moves as before.

### How it works
- The cashier came already rigged. The officer came as an unrigged model, so his skeleton and skin weights were borrowed from the cashier's rig and fitted to his size and proportions.
- Both new models are driven by the game's existing animation rig, so all the reactions, hit zones and the ragdoll still line up.
- Credits now list the model authors.
