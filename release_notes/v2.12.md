# v2.12: now on Godot 4.7, officers grip their pistols properly

**Download:** grab **JohnsBeerRun-Setup-v2.12.exe** (installer) or **JohnsBeerRun-Windows-v2.12.zip** below.

### Godot 4.7.2
- The whole game now runs on **Godot 4.7.2**, the same version as your editor. Until now it was built and tested on Godot 4.3, which is why some bugs showed up on your machine but not in my tests.
- The project files are converted to the 4.7 format, and the automatic release build now exports with 4.7.2.
- Every gameplay test (cabin, visitor, gun, driving, store, robbery and ragdolls, police chase, hiding, arrest, resisting, shootout, pause menu) was run on 4.7.2 with no errors.

### Officers hold their pistols properly
- **Proper two-handed grip.** Officers now hold the pistol the way real officers do: the shooting hand closed round the grip with the thumb up, the support hand wrapped over it, and the arms pushed out with a slight forward lean.
- **Posed on the model's own bones.** This is done on the police model's own arm, hand and finger bones, and the pistol is seated in the actual fist, so it moves with the hand.
- **What was wrong.** The arms were posed on the game's hidden animation rig and copied onto the police model. That rig's arms are about 7 cm longer, so the model's hands never reached the gun. On Godot 4.7 a tiny rounding error in the arm joints also built up every frame and twisted the elbows inward, which is the bent-arm, hands-at-the-chest look in your screenshots.
- **The gun stays drawn.** It stays up for the whole shootout, even when a pump or pillar briefly blocks an officer's view of John.

### Other fixes
- **Cruisers:** officers no longer climb onto them. They used to stand on the roof when one was in their way.
- **Officer's hands:** the police model's hands are refitted to their proper size (they had been stretched to match the jacket sleeves).
