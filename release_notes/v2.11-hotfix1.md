# v2.11 hotfix 1: parked cruisers, officers hold their guns

Same version (v2.11). Bug fixes only.

**Download:** grab **JohnsBeerRun-Setup-v2.11-hotfix1.exe** (installer) or **JohnsBeerRun-Windows-v2.11-hotfix1.zip** below.

### Fixes
- **Police cars kept driving after the officer got out.** A cruiser now brakes and stays put the moment its officer steps out, and waits there until he gets back in. In some modes (heading home, searching) cars used to drive off without him. If the officer is shot down, his cruiser stays where it stopped with its lights still flashing.
- **Officers didn't really hold their pistols.** The gun used to float near the wrist. Now officers aim in a proper two-handed stance: arms out, both hands meeting on the grip and fingers wrapped round it. The pistol sits in the officer's actual hand and moves with it.
- A cruiser could send a second officer out while its first was already on foot (when boxing in John's car). Fixed.
