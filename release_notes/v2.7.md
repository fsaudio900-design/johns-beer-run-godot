# v2.7: The Fuel Stop (stage 6)

**Download:** grab **JohnsBeerRun-v2.7.zip** below, unzip it, and import `project.godot` in Godot 4.3+.

### New
- **Money:** John starts with $500, shown in the HUD with +/− popups.
- **The fridge starts empty.** John has to make a beer run.
- **Dale the clerk:** he greets John ("Beer's in the back.") and turns to watch him around the store. The sliding doors open with a chime.
- **Buying a case:** grab a Lumberjack 12-pack from the stack in the back and pay at the register ($18.99).
  - Try to walk out without paying and Dale stops you.
  - Not enough money? He won't sell it.
- **Carrying it home:** John carries the case in both arms.
  - Put it in the **M1's trunk**: press E at the back of the car and the boot lid opens. Take it out the same way.
  - Press E at the fridge to stock it, for 12 more beers.

### The robbery
- **Hands up:** aim the Glock at Dale and he throws his hands up. Press E at the register to make him hand over the cash ($380–720).
- **Gunfire spooks him:** any shot in the store does it. A 90-second **Wanted** timer starts and a siren builds in the distance. The police themselves arrive in stage 7.
- **Hit reactions by body part:**
  - an arm hit spins him,
  - a leg hit drops him to one knee,
  - body hits make him hunch over and bleed.
- **Deaths:** three body hits or one headshot kills him.
  - He drops into a **real physics ragdoll** that crumples against the counter and shelves.
  - A blood pool spreads under him, with spray, splatter on the wall behind him, and wounds that stay on his body.
- **Rob the till yourself:** once Dale is dead, the way behind the counter opens up. Walk around, open the till and take the cash.
- **Stray bullets** knock products off the shelves, and they bounce and roll across the floor.

### Notes
- The blood decals use Godot's Forward+ renderer, which is the default on your PC.
