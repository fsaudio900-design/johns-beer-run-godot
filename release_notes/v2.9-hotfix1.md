# v2.9 hotfix 1: shooting fuel stop shelves

Same version (v2.9). Bug fix only.

**Download:** grab **JohnsBeerRun-Setup-v2.9-hotfix1.exe** (installer) or **JohnsBeerRun-Windows-v2.9-hotfix1.zip** below.

### Fix
- **Shooting the shelves spawned random items.** A shot used to create a few new boxes and cans with random colours that fell to the floor while the shelf stayed full. Now the item you actually hit (that box of chips, that bottle) is knocked off the shelf and falls with its own label, cap and colour, leaving a gap where it stood.
- You can shoot products inside the drink coolers too; bullets pass through the glass doors.
- Everything is restocked when you start a new game.
