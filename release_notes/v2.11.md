# v2.11: arrests, resisting, holster and police shootouts

**Download:** grab **JohnsBeerRun-Setup-v2.11.exe** (installer) or **JohnsBeerRun-Windows-v2.11.zip** below.

### Arrest animation
- When the police catch John, the officer draws his pistol and orders him to put his hands up. John turns to face him and raises both hands.
- The officer then holsters, walks round behind John and cuffs him (hands behind his back, cuffs click), reads him his rights, and it's **BUSTED**.

### Resist arrest
- If John has his Glock, a **"Resist (pull your gun)"** prompt appears during the arrest. Press **E** before the cuffs click and John spins round and draws on the officer, who's caught off guard for a moment.

### Holster (Z)
- Press **Z** to holster the Glock on John's hip, or press Z again to draw it. Right-click also draws it.

### Shootouts
- Once John resists, or shoots at or near the police, every officer draws and fires on sight. They keep their distance, back off if he gets close and chase him if he breaks line of sight.
- **John now has health.** It shows as a bar under the ammo counter and the screen edges turn red as he gets hurt. Moving makes him harder to hit, and he heals slowly after a few seconds without being shot. Getting shot down ends the night: **SHOT DOWN**.
- **Officers can be shot too.** A head shot or two body shots takes one down (ragdoll and blood); arms and legs take more. A cruiser whose officer is down can't make arrests.
