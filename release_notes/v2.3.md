# v2.3: The Glock (stages 1–4)

John's Beer Run, Godot 4 port.

**Download:** grab **JohnsBeerRun-v2.3.zip** below, unzip it, and import `project.godot` in Godot 4.3+. The first open spends a minute or two importing assets. Press F5 to play.

### What's in it
- **Stage 1:** the whole world (cabin, cul-de-sac, town with the police station, Fuel Stop) with lighting and collision, and John walking with his procedural animation.
- **Stage 2:** cabin life.
  - The recliner and drinking, the fridge, lines and the bong.
  - The trip with the flying cats, and the drunk, wired and trip screen effects.
  - The TV channels, the fire, the doors and the toilet.
  - The HUD, the title screen and the passed-out ending.
- **Stage 3:** the visitor walks up and knocks. Look through the peephole with **U**, open the door and he asks for number 14. He is invincible.
- **Stage 4:** the Glock.
  - Pick it up, aim with **RMB**, shoot with **LMB**, reload with **R**.
  - Bullet holes and sparks, the shattering bathroom mirror, and shooting the TV ends in Mission Failed.

### Fixes
- Aiming: you can now shoot and look around while holding RMB. The crosshair was swallowing the mouse.
