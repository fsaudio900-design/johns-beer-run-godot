# v2.8: The Pine Hollow police (stage 7)

**Download:** grab **JohnsBeerRun-v2.8.zip** below, unzip it, and import `project.godot` in Godot 4.3+.

### New
- **Cruisers:** one patrols Main Street and the cul-de-sac, and two wait in the station lot. Rob the Fuel Stop or fire your gun inside it, and all three light up and come for John.
- **Real pathfinding:** two navigation maps are built from the world when the game loads. One is for cruisers, which steer around houses, poles and buildings. The other is for officers on foot.
- **Officers on foot:** when a cruiser gets close, an officer jumps out and chases John. They run faster than John walks, so you have to break their line of sight. They'll follow you into the Fuel Stop.
- **Line of sight and searching:**
  - The Wanted pill shows **SPOTTED** while any cop can see John, and **SEARCHING** once they've lost him.
  - The Wanted clock only runs down while they can't see him.
  - When they lose him, the cruisers drive to where he was last seen and search the area. Their **roof spotlights sweep the yards**, and the officers search on foot.
- **Hiding in the cabin:** with the door shut, they stop at the porch, shout "Come out with your hands up!", and come in after about 6 seconds.
- **In the car:** if a cruiser boxes in the M1 while you're going slowly, John gets pulled out and arrested.
- **Getting caught** means **BUSTED**.
- **3D sirens:** every cruiser has its own siren. You can hear which direction it's coming from, it gets louder as it closes in, and it doppler-shifts as it drives past.
- **Lights:** red and blue light bars flash and wash over the houses and the road, and the cruisers have working headlights.

### Changed
- The distant 2D siren after a robbery is gone. The sirens now come from the actual cruisers.
