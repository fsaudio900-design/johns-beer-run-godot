# v2.20: mission marker and minimap

**Download:** grab **JohnsBeerRun-Setup-v2.20.exe** (installer) or **JohnsBeerRun-Windows-v2.20.zip** below.

### Mission marker at Ben's door
- A gold hologram stands in front of Ben's front door whenever he has a job for John: a beam of light with rising scan lines, a pulsing ring on the ground and a floating, flickering "!".
- It fades away as John walks up to the door, and is gone by the time he can knock.
- It disappears once John takes the job, and comes back when John has the money for Ben. It also shows again if John turns the job down, since it's still on offer.

### Minimap
- A round minimap in the bottom-left corner shows a map of the town: the cul-de-sac, Main Street and its buildings.
- It turns with the camera, so the top is always the direction you're looking. A small "N" on the rim shows north, and John's orange arrow in the middle points the way he's facing.
- **Ben's mission marker:** a gold "!" that pulses, and fades out as John gets close, the same as the hologram.
- **The bar:** a pink cocktail-glass symbol.
- **The Fuel Stop:** a green fuel-pump symbol.
- Markers out of range stick to the edge of the map, so you can always see which way to go.

Built and tested on Godot 4.7.2.
